# Certificate expiry is approaching

Certificate · Medium severity

## What it means

This certificate has between 15 and 30 days left before it expires — still a comfortable window, but close enough to be worth acting on rather than filing away.

## Why it matters

Nothing is wrong yet. This finding exists because "worth putting on a calendar" and "actually on a calendar" are different things, and certificate expiry is one of the most common causes of unplanned downtime precisely because it's invisible until the moment it isn't.

## How to fix it

Confirm renewal is actually automated for this certificate. If it is, no action needed beyond making sure the automation is monitored. If it isn't, book a specific day to renew it now rather than trusting a reminder to catch it later.
