# Domain registration expires soon

DNS · High severity

## What it means

The domain's registration expires between 15 and 45 days from now.

## Why it matters

A lapsed domain registration takes down the site, every subdomain, every certificate, and every email address tied to it — all at once, with no partial failure. Certificate expiry is recoverable in minutes; a lapsed domain is not. This finding exists to catch it well before it becomes urgent.

## How to fix it

Renew the domain registration with the registrar, and turn on auto-renewal so it doesn't need tracking manually going forward. Confirm the payment method and contact email on file are current — renewal failures are almost always caused by one of those two quietly going stale, not by anyone deciding not to renew.
